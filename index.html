Kavya Tamil Calendar - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Kavya Tamil Calendar ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Kavya Tamil Calendar mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Kavya Tamil Calendar does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, address, or email address to use the main features of the application.

Calendar data, Panchangam information, reminders, and application settings may be stored locally on your device where applicable.

2. Advertising

Kavya Tamil Calendar uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect or process certain information from your device, including device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising information, and information about interactions with advertisements, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable laws.

Advertisements may include banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and rewarded advertisements.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy

3. Notifications

The app may send calendar and reminder notifications if notification permission is enabled on your device.

You can control or disable notifications through the application's notification settings or your device settings.

4. Reminders

If you use the reminder functionality, reminder information may be stored locally on your device to provide the requested reminder service.

5. Rewarded Advertisements

The application may provide optional rewarded advertisements. When you voluntarily choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, the application may provide the corresponding in-app benefit.

You are not required to click advertisements in order to receive the benefit.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Kavya Tamil Calendar is a general-purpose calendar application. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

9. Data Deletion

Since the application does not require a user account, there is no account-based personal profile to delete.

Locally stored application data may be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application from your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
